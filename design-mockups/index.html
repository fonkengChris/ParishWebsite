Each direction is a working mockup of the home page, built with the real parish content. Every one wires into the parish's liturgical color system — use the season switcher in the top-right of each page to watch the whole design change with the Church's calendar.

IV. — Recommended

Communion × Sanctuary

The chosen hybrid: Communion's warm, welcoming home page as the base, with Sanctuary's dark arch-of-light borrowed as one dramatic "Come and pray with us" band. Welcome up top, awe in the middle, warmth throughout.

Warm base · one reverent, cinematic moment
I.

Breviary

Scholarly and quiet, like an illuminated missal. Parchment, Cormorant Garamond, gold hairlines, drop caps, and a vertical liturgical-color rail.

Editorial · Reverent · Text-forward
II.

Sanctuary

Immersive and cinematic — standing inside the church at dusk. Stone-dark, an arch of light, luminous Playfair type. The liturgical color glows like stained glass.

Dramatic · Modern · Photographic
III.

Communion

Warm, bright, and welcoming — the parish as a living community. Ivory and gold, soft Fraunces display, rounded-arch photo cards, generous whitespace.

Warm · Friendly · Approachable
